To open the AI-EOS USE Command

1. Open your PowerShell or Command Prompt.

2. First, switch to your D: drive by typing:

bash


D:


(Press Enter)

3. Next, navigate to the specific AI-EOS folder by typing:

bash


cd "D:\Project\AI Agent\artifacts\ai-eos"

(Press Enter)

4. Now that you are inside the correct folder, you can start the dashboard in development mode by running:

bash

pnpm dev    


(Press Enter)

This will automatically launch the real AI-EOS dashboard.
